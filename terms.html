Please read this first: DishDash is a student/academic software project built to demonstrate a food-ordering experience. No real restaurant, rider or payment gateway is involved. Every payment is simulated, no card is charged, and no food will be delivered. Nothing here creates a commercial contract for real goods or services.

1. About these terms

These terms describe the agreement between you and the DishDash demo ("DishDash", "we", "the site") when you browse, register or place an order. By using the site you accept them. If you do not accept them, please use the public pages only — or not at all.

DishDash exists to demonstrate software engineering: a multi-page storefront, an order lifecycle, a simulated payment stack and a separate admin console. It is not a live commercial service and should not be treated as one.

2. What is real and what is simulated

Being honest about this is the most important part of this document. Here is the split:

FeatureStatus
Accounts, sign-in and email confirmationReal — accounts work, and the confirmation code is genuinely sent when the cloud backend is connected. Seeded demo accounts are marked as already confirmed.
Menu, dishes, prices, cart and order historyReal — the data behaves like a working shop and persists between visits.
Order lifecycle and trackingReal mechanics, demo operator — an admin (not a real kitchen) moves each order through its stages.

Delivery, riders and ETAsSimulated — times shown are modelled estimates. No rider is dispatched and no food arrives.
Reviews, ratings and admin analyticsDemo data mixed with real entries — the seeded menu and some reviews ship with the project; orders and reviews you create are genuinely yours within the demo.

3. Accounts & verification

You need an account to place an order; browsing the homepage, menu and dish pages is free and does not require one.

  • Give accurate details. Your email address must be one you can open, because ordering is unlocked only after confirmation.
  • Phone numbers must be valid Nigerian mobile numbers in the accepted format — 11 digits beginning 070, 080, 081, 090 or 091.
  • Keep your sign-in to yourself. Anything done through your account is treated as done by you for the purposes of the demo.
  • Do not reuse a password you use anywhere else. In offline mode account records (including passwords) are kept in this browser's local storage rather than on a hardened server. Use a throwaway password.
  • The seeded demo accounts that ship with the project exist so the demo can be shown quickly; they are shared, public credentials and carry no privacy expectation.

We may suspend or clear an account that is used to abuse the site, spam reviews or disrupt the demo.

4. Orders, prices & availability

  • Prices are shown in Nigerian Naira (₦) and cover the food only. A flat delivery fee of ₦1,200 applies, and becomes free on subtotals of ₦20,000 or more.
  • Dishes can be marked unavailable or sold out by the operator; an order will not be accepted for an unavailable dish.
  • An order is a request, not a guarantee of food: it begins as Pending and is confirmed by the demo operator.
  • Order totals, promo discounts and receipts are calculated by the app and shown to you before you confirm — that figure is what the demo records.

5. Payment terms

No money ever changes hands. Every payment method on DishDash is a simulation built for study and demonstration.

  • Do not enter real card details. If you do, understand that they are not transmitted or stored by the app — but you should still only use made-up numbers, as you would for any public demo.
  • "Awaiting verification" means the operator has not yet confirmed the simulated payment; "Paid" means they have.
  • Bank-transfer details shown at checkout are fictional. Transfers to them will not reach anyone.
  • The operator may edit or delete demo orders at any time to keep the demonstration in a sensible state.

6. Delivery

Delivery is modelled for Lagos, city-wide. The site displays an estimated arrival — typically about 35 minutes from placement — and updates it as the order advances. These are illustrative estimates only: no rider is dispatched, no route is calculated and nothing will be delivered to your address. Delivery notes you type are stored on the order so the demo can show how special instructions travel with an order.

7. Cancellations & promo codes

  • You may cancel an order while it is still Pending, from the tracking page or from order details, optionally leaving a reason.
  • The operator may refuse an order at any point before delivery.
  • Cancelled orders are shown as cancelled with the reason and the person who called it off. They are excluded from revenue and statistics, and the order cannot be revived.
  • Every promo code is single-use per account — once an account has spent a code, that code cannot be used again on it. A code is spent when the order is placed, not when it is typed in. DISHWELCOME (₦1,500 off) carries an extra restriction: it is for a first order only. Cancelling the order that used a code releases it back to your account.
  • Refunds: once an order is paid and finished (delivered or cancelled) you may request a refund of the FULL order total from that order — one request per order, and it cannot be asked for again after a decision. An administrator approves or declines it by hand and their note appears on the order. Approving a refund removes the money from reported revenue and marks the order Refunded; declining it leaves the order paid.
  • Because DishDash simulates all payments, an approved refund is recorded and reflected in the figures but no money actually moves: there is no bank transfer, card reversal or gateway involved.

8. Reviews & feedback

Signed-in customers may review a dish once per account, and may edit or delete their own review at any time. Reviews you submit are visible to other visitors and to the admin console.

  • Keep reviews about the food, the service and your experience — not about other people.
  • Do not post anything unlawful, hateful, defamatory, spammy or containing personal data about someone else.
  • The "Verified order" badge means the reviewer has a delivered order containing that dish. It is not a guarantee of the reviewer's independence or honesty.
  • We may hide or delete reviews that break these rules, and may remove a review from an account that is abusing the feature.
  • Feedback sent through "Tell us how we're doing" is treated as project feedback and may be used to improve the demo.

10. Privacy & your data

DishDash stores as little as it can get away with, because it is a teaching project:

  • On the account: the name, email, phone number and delivery address you provide, plus your cart, favourites, orders and reviews.
  • Offline mode keeps all of that in this browser's local storage. It never leaves your device, and clearing site data removes it permanently.
  • Cloud mode (when the operator has connected a Supabase backend) stores the same records in the project's database so accounts work across browsers and devices, with sign-in sessions held by Supabase Auth and row-level security rules limiting who can read what.
  • Never stored anywhere: card numbers, CVV codes, expiry dates, and real bank account credentials. The account number used for the transfer name-enquiry is validated and used for that check only.
  • Deletion: clearing browser storage removes your offline demo data. In cloud mode, ask the operator to remove an account or its records. As a demonstration project, no formal retention schedule applies.
  • There are no advertising trackers, no third-party analytics scripts and no marketing email in this project.
