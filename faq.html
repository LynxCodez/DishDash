Ordering & your cart

How do I place an order?

Four steps: pick your food, review the cart, fill in checkout, choose how to pay.

  • Browse the menu, tap any dish, then Add to cart (the + button on a card works too).
  • Open the cart to change quantities, add a delivery note or drop a promo code.
  • At checkout confirm your delivery details and pick a payment method.
  • Place the order — you land on a confirmation page with an order number and an ETA.

You need to be signed in and have a confirmed email before the order can go through.

Browse the menu
Do I need an account just to look around?

No. The homepage, full menu, dish pages, search and this help area are open to everyone — no sign-in, no gate. You only need an account at the point of ordering, and signing out simply returns you to the public homepage.

What happens after I place an order?

Every order moves through the same five stages: Pending → Confirmed → Preparing → Out for delivery → Delivered. The kitchen team moves an order along from the admin console, and your tracking page updates the moment it does — including a small on-screen toast if you are browsing somewhere else on DishDash.

See my orders
Can I cancel an order?

Yes, while the order is still Pending — nothing has been cooked and no rider has left, so it can be stopped cleanly. Cancel from the tracking page or from the order details in My orders; you can add a reason (optional) so the team knows why.

After that, the kitchen can still refuse an order up to the moment it is delivered. Either way the order shows a red Cancelled state in your history with the reason and who called it off — it never silently disappears.

Cancelled orders stop counting towards revenue and statistics, and any promo code they had spent is handed back to your account.

Is there a minimum order?

No minimum. There is a flat delivery fee of ₦1,200, and it becomes free on orders of ₦20,000 or more — the cart shows a progress bar so you can see how close you are.

Payment

Which payment methods can I use?
  • Card — Visa, Mastercard or Verve, entered into an on-screen demo card.
  • Cash on delivery — pay the rider when the food arrives.
  • Bank transfer — transfer to the demo account shown and the team confirms it.
Will my card really be charged?

No. DishDash is an academic demo project: every payment method is simulated, no card details are stored or sent anywhere, and the "Demo Trust Bank" account is fictional. No real money moves — ever. That is why the footer carries the "payments are simulated" note.

What does "Awaiting verification" mean on my order?

Payment status is tracked separately from order status. For a transfer or cash on delivery order the money has not been confirmed yet, so it sits at Awaiting verification until the team confirms it from the admin console — at which point it flips to Paid. Card orders are marked paid immediately (the gateway is simulated).

How does the bank-account check work at checkout?

Pick your bank, type the 10-digit NUBAN account number, and DishDash runs a name enquiry. The account holder's name comes back and is shown to you as the paying account. You cannot finalise a transfer order until a name has been resolved — which mirrors how real Nigerian checkout flows behave.

Because this is a demo, the name enquiry falls back to a built-in resolver with sample accounts when no live provider key is configured. That fallback is what the seeded test accounts use.

Delivery

How long does delivery take?

Around 35 minutes on average. Your confirmation page and tracking page show a concrete clock time — "by 14:05", not a vague estimate — and it is recalculated from the moment the order was placed.

What does delivery cost?

₦1,200 flat, and free once your food subtotal reaches ₦20,000. The cart meter tells you exactly how much more is needed to unlock free delivery, and the fee appears as FREE in the totals and on your receipt.

Where do you deliver?

Lagos, city-wide — that is the coverage the demo is built around. Your address is remembered after each order so the next checkout is faster, and you can leave a delivery note (gate code, "ring twice", and so on) that the rider sees.

Account & verification

Why must I confirm my email before ordering?

Confirmation proves the address on the account is real. You can browse, favourite and fill your cart freely while unconfirmed — the gate is only at the moment an order is finalised, so nothing is lost while you sort it out.

We send a 6-digit code (and a confirmation link). The code is valid for 10 minutes, five wrong guesses retire it, and a fresh code can be requested after a short cooldown.

Verify my email
I never received the confirmation code.
  • Give it a minute or two, then check the spam or promotions folder.
  • Make sure you typed the address correctly when registering.
  • Request a new code — the previous one stops working once a fresh one is issued, and there is a short wait between requests.

The confirmation link in the email signs you straight in; if it has expired, request a new code and use the six digits instead.

Which phone numbers do you accept?

Nigerian mobile numbers only, and always the full 11 digits — written with or without spaces, e.g. 0803 412 7788. Accepted prefixes are 070, 080, 081, 090 and 091. Anything shorter, longer or starting differently is rejected with an inline explanation on the field — so a typo is caught before an order is placed.

I forgot my password.

This prototype has no password-reset email flow yet, so the quickest route is a fresh account with an email you can open, or one of the seeded demo accounts that ship with the project for exactly this purpose.

Also worth knowing: in offline demo mode passwords live only in this browser's local storage — the sign-in page warns you, so please don't reuse a real password here.

Promo codes & refunds

What promo codes exist, and how often can I use them?

Every promo code is one-time per account — once your account has used a code, that code is spent for good.

  • DISHWELCOME — ₦1,500 off, and it also has to be your first order.
  • FAST10 — 10% off orders over ₦5,000.

A code is spent at the moment the order is placed, not when you type it — so experimenting at checkout costs you nothing. If you cancel the order that used it, the code comes back.

The cart and checkout hint tell you the truth up front: a code you have already used shows as struck through with "already used on this account". Apply it there and the discount appears in the totals and on your receipt.

How do refunds work?

You ask, we decide. Open the order (from My orders or its tracking page) and choose Request a refund — you can say what went wrong, though it is optional. One request per order.

  • You can ask once the order is delivered or cancelled and its payment shows as Paid. A live order is stopped with Cancel order instead — that is a different tool.
  • The refund is for the full order total. There is no partial refund.
  • The support team approves or declines it by hand, and their note appears on the order with the amount and the date.
  • A decision is final — a declined refund cannot be asked for again.
  • An approved refund flips the order's payment badge to Refunded and takes the money out of what DishDash reports as revenue, so the numbers stay honest.

Because payments in this demo are simulated, no bank transfer of money actually happens — the workflow around it is real, the movement of funds is not.

Reviews & feedback

How do I leave a review?

Open any dish page while signed in and use the review panel — stars, an optional headline and a comment. One review per dish per account, and you can edit or delete your own later.

Reviews feed the rating chip on every dish card straight away, so a new review is visible on the menu without a refresh.

What is the "Verified order" badge on a review?

It means the reviewer has actually received that dish: we check your order history for a delivered order containing it. Where an admin can see the difference, reviews without that badge are clearly marked as unverified opinions.

Something went wrong — how do I tell you?

Use the "Tell us how we're doing" button in the footer of any page, or call and email us — the details are in the "Still stuck?" panel on this page and in the footer of every page.

If it is about a live order, have the order number to hand (it looks like DD-1014); that lets us find it instantly.
